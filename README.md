## 👋 About Me

I'm **Gayan Madushanka**, an undergraduate ICT student, designer, and content writer from Sri Lanka 🇱🇰. I bridge the gap between **technology and creativity** — building scalable software solutions, innovative design systems, and digitizing the local ecosystem.

- 🔭 Currently building **[Gamebadu.lk](https://gamebadulk.vercel.app/)** — a digital agricultural marketplace for Sri Lankan farmers
- 🎓 Studying **ICT** at university, focusing on software engineering & UI/UX
- 💡 Passionate about **eliminating rural digital barriers** and empowering local communities
- 🌱 Always learning and exploring new technologies

## 🚀 What I Do

- 💻 **Full-Stack Web Dev** — High-performance platforms using React, Vite, and Firebase.
- 📱 **Mobile Development** — Secure cross-platform apps with React Native & Expo.
- 🎨 **UI/UX & Design** — Responsive layouts, glassmorphism elements, design systems.
- ✍️ **Content Creation** — Compelling digital content and heritage storytelling.
